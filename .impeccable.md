## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, engineering peers, and potential collaborators reviewing the site on desktop and mobile. They need to understand Usama's experience, explore his projects and writing, and find a direct way to get in touch.

### Brand Personality
Confident, inventive, precise. Technical substance with a human voice.

### Aesthetic Direction
Complete visual overhaul requested October 2026. A kinetic portfolio with oversized Bricolage Grotesque typography, Geist body copy, cool neutral surfaces, a single cobalt accent, and generous asymmetric compositions. Light and dark themes follow the system unless the visitor chooses otherwise.

### Design Principles
1. Use coordinated text entrances, an interactive ribbon sculpture, responsive hover states, and native page transitions to communicate momentum.
2. Keep native scrolling, preserve readable static content, and respect reduced motion and explicit pause controls.
3. Let real project screenshots and substantial engineering content carry the story.
4. Preserve page URLs, article content, project destinations, résumé downloads, search, and keyboard access.
5. Apply the design system to every route, with responsive layouts down to 320px.

### Implementation
Astro with native CSS and small TypeScript modules. No client framework or animation dependency. Animation targets transform and opacity. Canvas animation is restricted to the visible hero and stops when the page is hidden or motion is paused.

Design dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8 / MOTION_INTENSITY 7 / VISUAL_DENSITY 3.
